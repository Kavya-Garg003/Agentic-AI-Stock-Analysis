Welcome to the **Agentic Stock Analysis Terminal**.

In high-stakes institutional finance, true "Autonomous Agents" (which blindly loop, guess, and iterate) are a liability—they are unpredictable, prone to hallucinations, and unacceptably slow.

To meet modern professional standards, this system relies on an Agentic Workflow. A central Orchestrator dictates a strict, deterministic, and highly reliable pipeline. It dispatches precise tasks to specialized sub-agents (Data Collection, FinBERT NLP, Quantitative Risk, and LLM Synthesis) to guarantee absolute accuracy every single time.
